# Code of conduct

sb2pb is maintained by a small team and used by people moving real projects. We want every issue,
pull request and discussion here to be a place where anyone can ask for help or contribute without
being talked down to.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. People write in their second or third language here.
- Discuss the code and the problem, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a change, and explain why when they do.
- Keep secrets out of public threads: no connection strings, keys or customer data in issues.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexual language or imagery, publishing someone's
private information, and repeated disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting
(Security → Report a vulnerability) and start the title with "Conduct:". Reports are read only by
the maintainers and handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn, ban
temporarily or ban permanently anyone who breaks these rules, in any project space.

Projects that want a more detailed policy often adopt the Contributor Covenant
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
